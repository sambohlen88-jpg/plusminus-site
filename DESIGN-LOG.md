| 2026-10-30 | Hero headline for both variants: "Websites for Perth businesses. Less fluff, more phone calls." The second sentence is set in mid grey. | It's the brief's third headline option. The hero's character comes from the ± field, not a slogan (owner feedback). The grey adds a + / − rhythm inside one line. | Pending |
| 2026-10-30 | Marks turn into − by collapsing the vertical arm and slightly widening the horizontal one. They don't rotate 90°. | A + rotated 90° looks identical, and part-way through the turn it reads as × ("close"). | Pending |
| 2026-10-30 | The ± logo is a 9 × 14 dot matrix. The plus is made of + marks drawn in bone, and the minus bar is made of − marks. | It reads as the logo at hero scale, and the minus half is literally made of minuses. | Pending |
| 2026-10-30 | Intro timing (A): marks converge 0.1–1.2s, the ± holds until 1.7s, then disperses by 2.6s. The headline starts rising at 1.6s, and the sub-line, buttons and promise strip follow 0.3–0.6s later. | The brief says the loader is under 1.2s and motion durations are 0.4–1.2s. | 2026-10-30 | Variant B keeps the ± formed on the right (centred top on mobile). Grid marks behind it are hidden, and on hover the logo's marks push about 22px away from the pointer and turn −. | It makes the ± the hero object rather than a passing intro. | Pending |
| 2026-10-30 | On touch screens the ambient wave draws − marks in bone at 40%, not lime. | Lime marks scattered behind text were too loud on mobile. | Pending |
| 2026-10-30 | Grid marks are color/line (#2E2E2E). A static SVG tile of the same grid shows when JavaScript is off or before the canvas starts, aligned so there's no jump. | The field should sit behind the type, not compete with it, and the hero must look right without JS. | Pending |
